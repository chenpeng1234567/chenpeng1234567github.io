# **四舵轮线性模型离散化**

在上面的运动学模型中，我们得到了：
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
\dot{X}=V \cos (\beta+\delta) \\
\dot{Y}=V \sin (\beta+\delta)\\
\dot{\psi}=\frac{V \cos \beta}{L_{f}+L_{r}}\left(\tan \theta_{1}-\tan \theta_{2}\right)
\end{array}\right.\tag{1}
$$
选取状态量为：![img](https://crpimg.oss-cn-wuhan-lr.aliyuncs.com/img/202407251639018.gif),控制量![img](https://crpimg.oss-cn-wuhan-lr.aliyuncs.com/img/202407251639029.gif)，对于参考轨迹上的任意参考点用r表示，即：
$$
\dot{X}=f(x, u) \stackrel{r}{\Rightarrow} \dot{X}_{r}=f\left(x_{r}, u_{r}\right) \\\tag{2}
$$
其中![img](https://crpimg.oss-cn-wuhan-lr.aliyuncs.com/img/202407251639796.gif)经过泰勒级数展开，并忽略高阶项：
$$
\dot{\chi}=f\left(\chi_{r}, \boldsymbol{u}_{r}\right)+\frac{\partial f(\chi, \boldsymbol{u})}{\partial \chi}\left(\chi-\chi_{r}\right)+\frac{\partial f(\chi, \boldsymbol{u})}{\partial \boldsymbol{u}}\left(\boldsymbol{u}-\boldsymbol{u}_{r}\right) \\\tag{3}
$$
对![img](https://crpimg.oss-cn-wuhan-lr.aliyuncs.com/img/202407251640486.gif)和![img](https://crpimg.oss-cn-wuhan-lr.aliyuncs.com/img/202407251640495.gif)求雅可比矩阵，有：
$$
\frac{\partial f(x, u)}{\partial x}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial \psi} \\
\frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial \psi} \\
\frac{\partial f_{3}}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial f_{3}}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial f_{3}}{\partial \psi}
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
0 & 0 & -v_{r \sin \left(\delta_{r}+\beta\right)} \\
0 & 0 & v_{r \cos \left(\delta_{r}+\beta\right)} \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right] \\
\frac{\partial f(x, u)}{\partial u}=\left[\begin{array}{ll}
\frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial v} & \frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial \theta} \\
\frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial v} & \frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial \theta} \\
\frac{\partial f_{3}}{\partial v} & \frac{\partial f_{3}}{\partial \theta}
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{cc}
\cos \left(\delta_{r}+\beta\right) & 0 \\
\sin \left(\delta_{r}+\beta\right) & 0 \\
\frac{\tan \theta_{1}-\tan \theta_{2}}{L} & \frac{v_{r}}{L \cos ^{2} \delta_{r}}
\end{array}\right] \\\tag{4}
$$
则，状态误差的变化量
$$
\dot{x}=\left[\begin{array}{c}
\dot{x}-\dot{x}_{r} \\
\dot{y}-y_{r} \\
\dot{\psi}-\dot{\psi}_{r}
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
0 & 0 & -v_{r s \sin \left(\delta_{r}+\beta\right)} \\
0 & 0 & v_{r \cos \left(\delta_{r}+\beta\right)} \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c}
x-x_{r} \\
y-y_{r} \\
\psi-\psi_{r}
\end{array}\right]+\left[\begin{array}{cc}
\cos \left(\delta_{r}+\beta\right) & 0 \\
\sin \left(\delta_{r}+\beta\right) & 0 \\
\frac{\tan \theta_{1}-\tan \theta_{2}}{L} & \frac{v_{r}}{L \cos ^{2} \delta_{r}}
\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{}
v-v_{r} \\
\theta-\theta_{r}
\end{array}\right] \tilde{u}(k)=\tilde{A} \tilde{x}(k)+\tilde{B} \tilde{u}(k) \\\tag{5}
$$
接下来进行线性离散化，
$$
\dot{\tilde{x}}(k+1)=(T\tilde{A}+I) \tilde{x}(k)+T\tilde{B} \tilde{u}(k) \\\tag{6}
$$
$$
\tilde{x}(k+1)=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & 0 & -T v_{r} \sin \psi_{r} \\
0 & 1 & T v_{r} \cos \psi_{r} \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right] \tilde{x}(k)+\left[\begin{array}{cc}
T \cos \psi_{r} & 0 \\
T \sin \psi_{r} & 0 \\
\frac{T\left(\tan \theta_{1}-\tan \theta_{2}\right)}{L} & \frac{v_{r} T}{L \cos ^{2} \delta_{r}}
\end{array}\right] \tilde{u}(k)=\tilde{A} \tilde{x}(k)+\tilde{B} \tilde{u}(k) \\\tag{7}
$$

其中，T为采样步长，I为单位矩阵，维度与A同。
